PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY REGISTRATION STEPS IN SRI LANKA

1. Reserve company name through the Registrar of Companies process.
2. Prepare incorporation documents.
3. Submit required forms and articles.
4. Obtain certificate of incorporation.
5. Register for tax and other regulatory needs after incorporation.

A private limited company is a separate legal entity.

Typical post-registration needs:
- TIN registration
- bank account opening
- local authority approvals where relevant
- employee registrations if staff are recruited
