TITLE: Pricing Guide for Home Bakers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: General Business Advisory | SME Pricing Principles

OVERVIEW
Correct pricing is critical for a sustainable home baking business.
Many home bakers underprice their products because they forget to include
all costs or undervalue their time.

THE BASIC PRICING FORMULA
Selling Price = Total Cost per Unit + Desired Profit Margin

STEP 1: CALCULATE INGREDIENT COST
List every ingredient used in one batch.
Calculate cost per gram or piece based on purchase price.
Divide total ingredient cost by the number of units produced.

Example for one dozen cupcakes:
- Flour 200g at LKR 200 per kg = LKR 40
- Sugar 150g at LKR 180 per kg = LKR 27
- Butter 100g at LKR 950 per 500g = LKR 190
- Eggs 2 at LKR 35 each = LKR 70
- Milk 100ml at LKR 120 per liter = LKR 12
- Baking powder, vanilla, salt = LKR 15
- Icing sugar and butter for frosting = LKR 80
Total ingredient cost for 12 cupcakes = LKR 434
Cost per cupcake = LKR 36

STEP 2: ADD OVERHEAD COSTS
- Electricity per batch: estimate LKR 30 to 80 depending on oven
- Packaging (box, wrapper, label) per unit: LKR 15 to 50
- Gas if applicable: estimate per batch
Overhead per cupcake (example) = LKR 20

STEP 3: ADD YOUR LABOR COST
Value your time honestly.
Example: if you take 2 hours to bake and decorate 12 cupcakes,
and you value your time at LKR 500 per hour:
Labor for 12 cupcakes = LKR 1000
Labor per cupcake = LKR 83

STEP 4: CALCULATE TOTAL COST
Total cost per cupcake = ingredient + overhead + labor
= LKR 36 + LKR 20 + LKR 83 = LKR 139

STEP 5: ADD PROFIT MARGIN
Recommended markup for home bakers: 30 to 60 percent
At 40 percent markup: Selling price = LKR 139 x 1.40 = LKR 195

ADDITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS
- If selling through a platform or agent who takes a commission, add that cost
- For custom orders, charge extra for design complexity
- Review prices regularly as ingredient costs change
- Do not compete only on price - focus on quality and unique offerings
