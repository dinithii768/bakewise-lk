TAX BASICS FOR SMALL FOOD BUSINESSES IN SRI LANKA

1. Small businesses should maintain sales and expense records.
2. A business may need a Taxpayer Identification Number.
3. VAT registration is generally linked to turnover thresholds.
4. Businesses should separate personal and business expenses clearly.
5. If workers are hired, payroll-related obligations may apply.
6. Food businesses selling online should still maintain proper records.
7. It is important to verify current tax rates and thresholds directly with IRD.
