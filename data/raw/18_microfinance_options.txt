TITLE: Microfinance Options for Home Food Entrepreneurs in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Central Bank of Sri Lanka | Microfinance Industry Advisory

OVERVIEW
Microfinance provides small loans to entrepreneurs who do not yet qualify
for conventional bank loans. It is a practical starting point for home
food sellers who need startup capital.

KEY MICROFINANCE PROVIDERS IN SRI LANKA

1. Sarvodaya Economic Enterprise Development Services (SEEDS)
   - Community-based microfinance
   - Group lending model encourages peer accountability
   - Loan range: LKR 25,000 to LKR 500,000
   - Interest rates: approximately 18 to 24 percent per annum

2. LOLC Micro Credit
   - Largest private microfinance provider
   - Individual and group loans
   - Loan range: LKR 50,000 to LKR 2,500,000
   - Available island-wide

3. Berendina Microfinance Institute
   - Rural and peri-urban focus
   - Women-centered lending programs

4. National Development Trust Fund
   - Government-backed microfinance
   - Lower interest rates than commercial microfinance

GROUP LENDING MODEL EXPLAINED
- A group of five to ten entrepreneurs forms a loan group
- All members guarantee each other's loans
- One member defaults and others are responsible
- Encourages responsible borrowing and repayment

WHEN TO USE MICROFINANCE
- When you need LKR 25,000 to LKR 500,000 to start
- When you do not yet have collateral for a bank loan
- When you want to build your credit history before approaching banks
- When you need flexible repayment terms

PREPARING FOR YOUR MICROFINANCE APPLICATION
- Have your NIC ready
- Prepare a simple description of your business
- Estimate your monthly income and expenses
- Show any existing savings as a positive sign
