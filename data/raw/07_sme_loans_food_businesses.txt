TITLE: SME Loan Options for Food Businesses in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Central Bank of Sri Lanka | Government Credit Schemes | www.cbsl.gov.lk

OVERVIEW
Food entrepreneurs in Sri Lanka have access to several financing options
from government schemes and commercial banks.

GOVERNMENT CREDIT SCHEMES

1. Enterprise Sri Lanka Loan Scheme
   - Ran Aswenna: Agriculture and food production at 4 percent interest
   - Sthree Shakthiya: Women entrepreneurs at 4 percent interest
   - Gami Pubuduwa: Rural enterprise development
   - Available through participating commercial banks

2. SMEDA (Small and Medium Enterprise Development Authority)
   - Business development support
   - Advisory services
   - Training and capacity building

COMMERCIAL BANK OPTIONS

Bank of Ceylon
   - SME loans up to LKR 50 million
   - Working capital and term loans available

Peoples Bank
   - Mihijaya loan scheme for SMEs
   - Competitive interest rates

Regional Development Bank
   - Focus on rural and semi-urban entrepreneurs
   - Smaller loan amounts suitable for startups

MICROFINANCE OPTIONS
- Sarvodaya SEEDS
- LOLC Micro Credit
- Loan range: LKR 25,000 to LKR 2,500,000
- Suitable for very early stage home food businesses

TYPICAL REQUIREMENTS FOR LOANS
- Business registration documents
- National Identity Card
- Bank statements for six months
- Business plan or cash flow projection
- Collateral or guarantor for larger amounts
- Clean credit record at CRIB (Credit Information Bureau)

PRACTICAL TIPS
- Start building your credit record early
- Maintain clean financial records from day one
- Prepare a simple business plan before approaching a bank
- Consider microfinance first if you are just starting out
