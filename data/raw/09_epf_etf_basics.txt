TITLE: EPF and ETF Basics for Food Business Owners Who Hire Staff
SOURCE: Department of Labour Sri Lanka | Employees Provident Fund | www.labourdept.gov.lk

OVERVIEW
If you hire any paid employees in your food business, you are legally required
to register for EPF and ETF and make monthly contributions.

EPF - EMPLOYEES PROVIDENT FUND
- Employee contribution: 8 percent of gross salary
- Employer contribution: 12 percent of gross salary
- Total contribution: 20 percent of gross salary per employee
- Managed by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka

ETF - EMPLOYEES TRUST FUND
- Employer contribution only: 3 percent of gross salary
- No deduction from employee salary
- Managed by the Employees Trust Fund Board

REGISTRATION PROCESS
1. Register with the Employees Provident Fund within 14 days of hiring the first employee
2. Register with the Employees Trust Fund Board
3. Set up monthly payroll to deduct employee EPF contributions
4. Make combined EPF and ETF payments monthly

EXAMPLE CALCULATION
If you pay a helper LKR 30,000 per month:
- Employee EPF deduction: LKR 30,000 x 8 percent = LKR 2,400
- Employer EPF payment: LKR 30,000 x 12 percent = LKR 3,600
- Employer ETF payment: LKR 30,000 x 3 percent = LKR 900
- Total employer additional cost: LKR 4,500 per month
- Employee takes home: LKR 30,000 minus LKR 2,400 = LKR 27,600

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE
- Penalties and surcharges for late payments
- Legal action by the Department of Labour
- Employees can file complaints and claim unpaid contributions
