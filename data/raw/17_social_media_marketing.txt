TITLE: Social Media Marketing Tips for Home Food Sellers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Digital Business Advisory | General Marketing Principles

OVERVIEW
Social media is the most cost-effective marketing tool for home food sellers
in Sri Lanka. Facebook and Instagram are the dominant platforms.

SETTING UP YOUR BUSINESS PROFILE

Facebook
- Create a Facebook Business Page separate from personal profile
- Add complete business information including contact number
- Set up the Shop or Services section if applicable
- Enable Messenger for quick customer inquiries

Instagram
- Switch to a Business or Creator account for analytics
- Write a clear bio stating what you sell and where you deliver
- Add a contact button
- Use Instagram Shopping features if available

WhatsApp Business
- Create a WhatsApp Business account
- Set up automated replies for greetings and away messages
- Create a product catalog in WhatsApp Business
- Use broadcast lists to notify regular customers of new items

CONTENT STRATEGY

Photos
- Use natural light for best results
- Style your food on simple clean backgrounds
- Show the inside of products where relevant
- Consistent visual style builds brand recognition

Posts to Create Regularly
- New product announcements
- Behind the scenes baking content
- Customer testimonials with permission
- Seasonal and festival specials
- Limited time offers to create urgency

Captions
- Always list allergens in product descriptions
- Include price and ordering details
- Add relevant hashtags in Sinhala and English

GROWING YOUR AUDIENCE
- Ask satisfied customers to tag you in their posts
- Collaborate with other non-competing home businesses
- Run occasional giveaways to grow followers
- Reply to all comments and messages promptly
