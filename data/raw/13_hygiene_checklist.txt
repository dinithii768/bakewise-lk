TITLE: Kitchen Hygiene Checklist for Home Food Sellers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Ministry of Health Sri Lanka | Public Health Inspectorate Guidelines

DAILY HYGIENE CHECKLIST

Before Starting:
- Wash hands thoroughly with soap for at least 20 seconds
- Tie back hair or wear a hair net
- Remove jewelry from hands and wrists
- Wear clean apron dedicated to food preparation
- Clean and sanitize all work surfaces
- Check all utensils and equipment are clean

During Preparation:
- Wash hands after touching phone, face, or non-food items
- Keep raw ingredients separate from ready-to-eat products
- Use separate chopping boards for different food types
- Check ingredients for freshness before use
- Monitor oven and cooking temperatures

After Completion:
- Clean all equipment and surfaces immediately
- Wash all utensils with hot soapy water
- Sanitize countertops with food-safe sanitizer
- Dispose of waste properly in sealed bags
- Sweep and mop the kitchen floor
- Check refrigerator temperatures are correct

WEEKLY TASKS
- Deep clean oven removing grease and residue
- Clean refrigerator shelves and door seals
- Check expiry dates on all stored ingredients
- Wash reusable storage containers
- Check pest control measures are in place

PEST CONTROL
- Seal gaps in walls and around pipes
- Store all ingredients in sealed containers
- Do not leave food waste exposed overnight
- Use approved pest control methods if needed
- Report rodent or pest sightings immediately

IMPORTANT REMINDER
A surprise PHI inspection can happen at any time.
Keeping your kitchen consistently clean protects your customers,
your reputation, and your business.
