TITLE: Food Labeling Requirements for Sri Lankan Food Products
SOURCE: Sri Lanka Standards Institution | Consumer Affairs Authority

OVERVIEW
Food labels protect consumers and build trust in your brand.
In Sri Lanka, labeling requirements are governed by the Food Act and
Sri Lanka Standards Institution regulations.

MANDATORY LABEL INFORMATION

1. Product Name
   - Clear and accurate name of the food product
   - Should not be misleading

2. Ingredient List
   - List all ingredients in descending order of weight
   - Include water if used as an ingredient
   - Use common names for ingredients

3. Allergen Declaration
   - Clearly highlight the following allergens if present:
     * Gluten-containing grains (wheat, barley, rye)
     * Milk and dairy products
     * Eggs
     * Nuts including peanuts
     * Soy
     * Fish and shellfish
     * Sesame seeds
   - Allergens should be emphasized using bold or highlighted text

4. Net Weight or Volume
   - State the net quantity of the product
   - Use grams or kilograms for solid foods
   - Use milliliters or liters for liquids

5. Manufacturer Details
   - Name and address of the manufacturer or packer
   - For home businesses: your name and address or business name

6. Date Marking
   - Best Before date for products that degrade slowly
   - Use By date for perishable products that could become unsafe after that date
   - Format recommended: DD/MM/YYYY

7. Storage Instructions
   - State how the product should be stored
   - Example: Store in a cool dry place, Refrigerate after opening

8. Batch or Lot Number
   - Useful for traceability in case of a recall

RECOMMENDED ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
- Nutritional information panel (not always mandatory for small producers but builds trust)
- Serving suggestion
- QR code linking to more product information

PRACTICAL TIPS FOR HOME BAKERS
- Design professional looking labels even for home-baked items
- Print clearly readable text on labels
- Use food-safe label materials
- Check labels before each batch to ensure dates are correct
