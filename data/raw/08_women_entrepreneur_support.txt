TITLE: Support for Women Entrepreneurs in Sri Lanka Food Sector
SOURCE: Ministry of Industry | SMEDA | Central Bank of Sri Lanka

OVERVIEW
The Sri Lankan government and financial institutions provide specific support
for women who run food businesses, including home-based operations.

KEY SUPPORT PROGRAMS

1. Sthree Shakthiya Loan Scheme
   - Specifically designed for women entrepreneurs
   - Interest rate: 4 percent per annum (concessionary)
   - Available through participating commercial banks
   - Purpose: working capital or investment in equipment

2. Women Entrepreneurship Promotion Programs by SMEDA
   - Free training on business management
   - Marketing support
   - Linkage to buyers and markets

3. National Enterprise Development Authority (NEDA)
   - Capacity building programs
   - Mentorship for women entrepreneurs

4. Export Development Board Support
   - For women in food export
   - Trade fair participation support
   - Market development assistance

PRACTICAL BENEFITS FOR HOME BAKERS
- Lower interest rates reduce monthly loan repayments significantly
- Training helps improve business management skills
- Government programs provide credibility when approaching buyers
- Networking through these programs opens new sales channels

HOW TO APPLY FOR STHREE SHAKTHIYA
1. Visit a participating commercial bank
2. Request the Sthree Shakthiya or Enterprise Sri Lanka application
3. Submit required documents including business registration and NIC
4. Provide a simple business plan
5. The bank will assess and process the application
