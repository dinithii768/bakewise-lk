TITLE: Consumer Rights and CAA Regulations for Food Sellers
SOURCE: Consumer Affairs Authority Sri Lanka | www.caa.gov.lk

OVERVIEW
The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) protects buyers from unfair practices
and ensures businesses comply with pricing and quality standards.

KEY OBLIGATIONS FOR FOOD SELLERS

1. Price Display
   - Prices must be clearly displayed on products or menus
   - Charging more than displayed price is prohibited
   - Price changes must be communicated before sale is confirmed

2. Accurate Representation
   - Do not misrepresent the ingredients, size, or quality of products
   - Photos and descriptions used in marketing must reflect the real product
   - False claims about health benefits are prohibited

3. Honest Weight and Measure
   - Products sold by weight must be accurately weighed
   - Packaging should not mislead about actual contents

4. Product Safety
   - You must not sell food that is unsafe or unfit for consumption
   - Expired products must not be sold
   - Damaged or compromised packaging should not be offered for sale

CONSUMER COMPLAINT PROCESS
- Consumers can complain to CAA through hotline 1977
- Online complaints available at www.caa.gov.lk
- CAA can investigate and take action against sellers

PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS
- Fines up to LKR 500,000
- Business closure orders
- Criminal prosecution for serious offenses

BEST PRACTICE TIPS
- Issue receipts or digital payment confirmations for all sales
- Have a clear and fair refund policy for genuinely damaged items
- Respond professionally to complaints rather than ignoring them
- Keep records of all orders and sales for at least one years
