TITLE: Online Selling Guidelines for Home Food Businesses in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Consumer Affairs Authority | Electronic Transactions Act No. 19 of 2006

OVERVIEW
Many home food sellers in Sri Lanka use Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp
to take orders and sell products. This guide covers best practices and
legal considerations for online food selling.

KEY PLATFORMS USED BY SRI LANKAN HOME FOOD SELLERS
- Facebook Pages and Groups
- Instagram Business Profiles
- WhatsApp Business
- Daraz.lk marketplace
- Kapruka.com marketplace

LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS

1. Business Registration
   - Even online sellers should be registered as a business
   - Using your personal name or a trade name is acceptable for sole proprietors

2. Consumer Protection
   - Clearly display your prices
   - Honor orders once confirmed
   - Provide a clear refund or replacement policy for damaged items
   - Do not post misleading photos of your products

3. Accurate Descriptions
   - List all ingredients especially allergens
   - State shelf life and storage requirements in your posts or messages
   - Be honest about what is homemade versus sourced from a third party

4. Payment Handling
   - Offer multiple payment options: bank transfer, mobile payment apps
   - Send payment confirmations to customers
   - Keep records of all transactions

BEST PRACTICES FOR SOCIAL MEDIA FOOD SELLING
- Use real, high-quality photos of your actual products
- Post consistently to build a following
- Respond to inquiries quickly
- Request reviews and testimonials from happy customers
- Be transparent about your home kitchen setup to build trust

DELIVERY TIPS
- Set clear delivery areas and charges
- Use reliable couriers for deliveries outside your local area
- Communicate estimated delivery times clearly
- Follow up with customers after delivery
