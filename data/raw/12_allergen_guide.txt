TITLE: Allergen Guide for Home Bakers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Food Safety Advisory | Consumer Affairs Authority Sri Lanka

OVERVIEW
Food allergies can cause serious health reactions in some customers.
As a home baker, you have a responsibility to clearly communicate
allergen information.

THE MAJOR ALLERGENS TO DECLARE

1. Gluten
   - Found in: wheat, barley, rye, some oats
   - Common in: cakes, cookies, bread, pastries
   - Alternative: gluten-free flour blends available in Sri Lanka

2. Dairy
   - Found in: butter, milk, cream, cheese, yogurt
   - Common in: most buttercream, cream cakes, milk-based desserts
   - Alternative: coconut milk, vegan butter for dairy-free versions

3. Eggs
   - Found in: most baked goods as binding agent
   - Alternative: flax egg, chia egg, aquafaba for vegan options

4. Tree Nuts
   - Common nuts used: cashew, almond, walnut, pistachio
   - Must be declared even in trace amounts

5. Peanuts
   - Declared separately from tree nuts
   - High-risk allergen with potentially severe reactions

6. Soy
   - Found in: some margarines, chocolate, soy flour

7. Sesame
   - Found in: sesame seeds, tahini, some bread toppings

HOW TO DECLARE ALLERGENS
- List all allergens clearly in your product descriptions online
- Print allergen information on labels
- Use phrases like: Contains: Gluten, Dairy, Eggs, Tree Nuts
- For may contain traces: Made in a kitchen that handles peanuts

HANDLING ALLERGEN-FREE ORDERS
- Clean all equipment thoroughly before preparing allergen-free orders
- Use separate utensils and baking trays where possible
- Be honest if you cannot guarantee allergen-free conditions
- Never hide allergens from customers to make a sale
