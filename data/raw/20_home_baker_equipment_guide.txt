TITLE: Equipment Guide for Home Bakers Starting a Food Business in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: General Baking Industry Advisory

OVERVIEW
Having the right equipment helps you produce consistent quality products
efficiently. This guide covers essential and optional equipment for home bakers
in Sri Lanka starting a food business.

ESSENTIAL EQUIPMENT

1. Oven
   - Countertop convection oven is suitable for home production
   - Capacity: 30 to 60 liters suits most home bakeries
   - Brands available in Sri Lanka: Abans, Singer, Panasonic, Butterfly
   - Approximate cost: LKR 25,000 to LKR 80,000

2. Mixer
   - Hand mixer: affordable starting point at LKR 3,000 to 8,000
   - Stand mixer: more efficient for larger volumes at LKR 25,000 to 60,000
   - Essential for consistent mixing of batters and creams

3. Baking Tins and Trays
   - Invest in good quality non-stick tins
   - Round, square, loaf, and cupcake tins are most versatile
   - Approximate cost: LKR 500 to 2,000 per piece

4. Weighing Scale
   - Digital scales essential for accurate recipes
   - Available at LKR 1,500 to 5,000 in Sri Lanka

5. Refrigerator
   - Dedicated space in refrigerator for food products
   - Separate raw ingredients from finished products
   - Upright display fridge useful as business grows

6. Packaging Materials
   - Cake boxes in various sizes
   - Food bags and wrapping
   - Labels and stickers
   - Tissue paper and ribbon for premium presentation

GROWTH EQUIPMENT (when volume increases)
- Commercial grade mixer
- Additional oven for higher volume
- Chest freezer for storing batches
- Shrink wrap machine for professional sealing

WHERE TO BUY IN SRI LANKA
- Baking ingredient shops in Pettah, Colombo
- Abans and Singer showrooms for appliances
- Online through Daraz and Kapruka for smaller items
- Wholesale markets for packaging materials in bulk

MAINTENANCE TIPS
- Clean oven after every use to prevent smoke and flavor transfer
- Service stand mixer annually to keep it running smoothly
- Replace non-stick coatings when they begin to wear
