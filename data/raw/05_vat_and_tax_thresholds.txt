TITLE: VAT and Tax Thresholds for Small Food Businesses in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Inland Revenue Department Sri Lanka | www.ird.gov.lk

INCOME TAX FOR SMALL BUSINESSES

Individual (Sole Proprietor) Tax Rates 2024:
- First LKR 1,200,000 of profit: Tax free
- LKR 1,200,001 to 1,700,000: 6 percent
- LKR 1,700,001 to 2,200,000: 12 percent
- LKR 2,200,001 to 2,700,000: 18 percent
- LKR 2,700,001 to 3,200,000: 24 percent
- Above LKR 3,200,000: 30 percent

Company Tax Rate:
- Standard corporate tax rate: 30 percent
- Small and medium enterprise rate may be lower depending on qualifying conditions

VAT (VALUE ADDED TAX)

VAT Registration Threshold:
- A business must register for VAT if quarterly turnover exceeds LKR 15,000,000
- Or if annual turnover exceeds LKR 60,000,000
- Most home food sellers will be BELOW this threshold initially

VAT Rate:
- Standard rate: 18 percent on taxable supplies
- Certain food items may be exempt from VAT

Zero-Rated and Exempt Foods:
- Unprocessed agricultural produce is generally exempt
- Processed and packaged foods are generally standard-rated at 18 percent
- Check with IRD for specific product classification

WHAT SMALL HOME FOOD SELLERS NEED TO DO
1. Obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) from IRD
2. Maintain records of all income and expenses
3. File an income tax return annually
4. Register for VAT only when turnover exceeds the threshold
5. If unsure, consult an accountant familiar with Sri Lankan tax law
