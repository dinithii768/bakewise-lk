TITLE: Business Registration Basics for Home Food Sellers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Department of Registrar of Companies Sri Lanka | www.drc.gov.lk

OVERVIEW
Home-based food businesses in Sri Lanka can operate under several legal structures.
Choosing the right structure depends on the size, goals, and number of owners.

BUSINESS STRUCTURE OPTIONS

1. Sole Proprietorship
   - Simplest and cheapest to start
   - Registered at the Divisional Secretariat
   - Owner is personally liable for all debts
   - Registration fee: approximately LKR 600 to 1500
   - Renewal: annually
   - Required documents: National Identity Card, proof of address, chosen business name

2. Partnership
   - Two to twenty partners allowed
   - Registered under the Partnership Ordinance
   - All partners share liability
   - No separate legal identity from the owners

3. Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)
   - Registered under Companies Act No. 7 of 2007
   - Minimum one director and one shareholder
   - Separate legal entity which protects personal assets
   - Registration fee approximately LKR 5415
   - Can be done online at www.drc.gov.lk
   - Timeline: one to three working days for online applications

RECOMMENDATION FOR HOME FOOD SELLERS
Most home bakers and food sellers start as sole proprietors.
This allows them to test the market before committing to more complex structures.
As the business grows and hires staff or takes on investors, converting to a private
limited company becomes more practical.

POST REGISTRATION STEPS
- Apply for Taxpayer Identification Number at the Inland Revenue Department
- Obtain a trade license from the local municipal council or pradeshiya sabha
- Register for EPF and ETF if hiring any employees
- Open a separate business bank account for clear financial records
