TITLE: Trade License Requirements for Food Businesses in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Local Government Authorities | Municipal Councils | Pradeshiya Sabhas

OVERVIEW
A trade license or business permit is required from the local authority
in the area where the business operates. For home-based food businesses,
this means the Municipal Council, Urban Council, or Pradeshiya Sabha
covering your residential area.

TYPES OF LOCAL AUTHORITIES
- Colombo Municipal Council for Colombo city
- Other Municipal Councils for major cities
- Urban Councils for smaller towns
- Pradeshiya Sabhas for rural and semi-urban areas

STEPS TO OBTAIN A TRADE LICENSE

1. Visit or contact your local authority office
2. Obtain the application form for a trade license or business permit
3. Submit required documents:
   - Completed application form
   - Copy of National Identity Card
   - Proof of address of the business premises
   - Deed or rental agreement for the premises
   - For food businesses: sometimes a PHI clearance is required
4. Pay the applicable fee
5. Await inspection if required
6. Receive trade license certificate

LICENSE FEES
Fees vary by local authority and business type.
Approximate range: LKR 1000 to 10000 per year for small food businesses.
Fees are subject to annual renewal.

IMPORTANT NOTES FOR HOME FOOD SELLERS
- Operating from home may require permission from the local authority
- Some areas have zoning restrictions on commercial activity in residential zones
- Check with your local authority before starting commercial production at home
- Renew your trade license on time to avoid penalties

CONSEQUENCES OF OPERATING WITHOUT A LICENSE
- Fines from the local authority
- Forced closure of operations
- Difficulty opening business bank accounts
- Challenges when applying for business loans
