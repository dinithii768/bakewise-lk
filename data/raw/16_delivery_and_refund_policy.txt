TITLE: Delivery and Refund Policy Guide for Home Food Sellers
SOURCE: General Consumer Best Practice Advisory

DELIVERY POLICY ELEMENTS TO DEFINE

1. Delivery Area
   - Define which areas you deliver to
   - State which areas require extra charges
   - Be clear about areas you do not cover

2. Delivery Timeframe
   - State estimated delivery windows
   - Example: Colombo same day if ordered before 12 noon
   - Give realistic estimates - do not overpromise

3. Delivery Charges
   - Clearly state delivery fees upfront
   - Free delivery above a minimum order amount encourages larger orders
   - Consider platform delivery apps for wider reach

4. Order Lead Time
   - For custom cakes: minimum 48 to 72 hours notice
   - For standard items: 24 hours minimum
   - For large bulk orders: one week minimum

REFUND AND REPLACEMENT POLICY

When to offer refunds or replacements:
- Product arrived damaged due to delivery
- Product significantly different from description
- Wrong product sent

When refunds or replacements are generally not offered:
- Customer changed their mind after delivery
- Minor cosmetic variations in handmade products
- Customer failed to store correctly after delivery

HOW TO HANDLE COMPLAINTS PROFESSIONALLY
1. Listen to the customer complaint without being defensive
2. Ask for a photo of the issue
3. Assess whether replacement or refund is appropriate
4. Resolve quickly to protect your reputation
5. Learn from feedback to improve your products

IMPORTANT NOTE
Your refund policy should be stated clearly on your social media profiles
and shared with customers when they place an order.
