TITLE: Packaging and Storage Guidelines for Home Food Sellers in Sri Lanka
SOURCE: Sri Lanka Standards Institution | Consumer Affairs Authority

OVERVIEW
Proper packaging and storage protect food quality, extend shelf life,
and build customer trust in your home food business.

PACKAGING REQUIREMENTS

1. Food-Safe Materials
   - Use packaging materials that are approved for food contact
   - Avoid recycled plastic bags or newspaper for direct food contact
   - Use food-grade plastic containers, boxes, or wrapping

2. Seal and Tamper Evidence
   - Sealed packaging reassures customers about hygiene
   - Heat-sealed bags or shrink wrap protect baked goods
   - Stickers or tape seals show if packaging has been opened

3. Label Placement
   - Attach labels securely so they do not fall off during delivery
   - Ensure all label information is visible and readable

4. Portion Control
   - Clear packaging makes portion sizes obvious to customers
   - Individual wrapping for items like cookies builds perceived value

STORAGE GUIDELINES

Room Temperature Storage
- Suitable for: most dry baked goods, cookies, brownies, bread
- Keep in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- Average shelf life: 2 to 5 days depending on ingredients

Refrigerated Storage
- Suitable for: cream-filled cakes, cheesecakes, fresh cream items
- Store at 4 degrees Celsius or below
- Keep away from raw meat and strong-smelling foods
- Shelf life: 3 to 7 days depending on ingredients

Frozen Storage
- Suitable for: unbaked dough, certain finished products
- Store at minus 18 degrees Celsius
- Label with freezing date
- Shelf life: 1 to 3 months for most baked items

DELIVERY CONSIDERATIONS
- Use insulated bags for cream or chocolate products in hot weather
- Pad packaging to prevent damage during delivery
- Mark boxes as fragile for delicate cakes
- Communicate storage instructions to customers clearly
