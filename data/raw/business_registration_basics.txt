BUSINESS REGISTRATION BASICS FOR SMALL FOOD BUSINESSES IN SRI LANKA

1. A home-based food business can operate as:
- Sole proprietorship
- Partnership
- Private limited company

2. Sole proprietorship is usually the simplest starting point for a single owner.

3. Private limited company is useful when:
- there are multiple owners
- liability protection is needed
- the business wants to scale formally

4. Key registration-related authorities:
- Department of Registrar of Companies
- Local authority / municipal council / pradeshiya sabha
- Inland Revenue Department

5. Important follow-up registrations may include:
- Taxpayer Identification Number
- trade license depending on local authority requirements
- EPF/ETF if employees are hired
